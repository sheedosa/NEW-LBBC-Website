The UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) has updated its travel advice for Libya, advising against all but essential travel to all areas within the city limits of Tripoli. This brings Tripoli into line with Benghazi and Misrata, meaning all three cities are now subject to the same advisory level under current FCDO guidance. The update was published on 15 June 2026.

Under the revised advice, the FCDO continues to advise against all travel to the rest of Libya, while maintaining all but essential travel guidance for Tripoli, Benghazi and Misrata. The guidance also notes that UK government support in Libya is limited, with consular assistance for British nationals provided through the British Embassy in Tunis rather than in person in Libya.

For businesses and travellers, the change restores Tripoli on the same footing as Benghazi and Misrata in the UK's travel risk framework. As reported by *Libya Herald*, the revision is likely to be viewed as a positive step for business travel and commercial engagement, particularly given Tripoli's central role in government and business activity.

The LBBC advises members to review the latest official guidance carefully before travelling, ensure robust security arrangements and contingency planning are in place, and monitor the [FCDO advice page](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/libya) for any further updates.
