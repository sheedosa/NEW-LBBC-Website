The Libyan British Business Council is delighted to announce that the National Oil Corporation of Libya (NOC) and the LBBC have signed a landmark Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) during the Libya Energy Forum held in London on 13 May 2026.

This strategic partnership represents a significant milestone in strengthening the relationship between Libya's national oil company and the UK business community. The MoU establishes a framework for enhanced dialogue, knowledge exchange, and collaborative initiatives focused on Libya's energy sector development and international business engagement.

The signing ceremony, attended by HE Masoud Suleiman, Chairman of the NOC, along with senior leadership from both organisations, took place during a full day of strategic dialogue and networking at the Libya Energy Forum. The event brought together senior Libyan officials, international oil companies, investors, and technical partners to discuss Libya's energy opportunities and the country's positioning within the global energy landscape.

Key areas of collaboration outlined in the MoU include market intelligence sharing, facilitation of business networking opportunities, support for sector-focused engagement initiatives, and joint efforts to strengthen the UK-Libya business relationship. The partnership aims to create new channels for dialogue on market entry, project opportunities, and the practical conditions needed to support long-term business engagement in Libya.

This agreement underscores the growing interest in Libya's upstream portfolio, gas development opportunities, and the broader energy sector potential. The LBBC and the NOC look forward to working together to create opportunities for their respective members and stakeholders to engage meaningfully on Libya's energy priorities.

For further information about this partnership or Libya Energy Forum outcomes, please contact the LBBC Secretariat at events@lbbc.org.uk
