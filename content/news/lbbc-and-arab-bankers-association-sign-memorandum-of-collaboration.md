The Libyan British Business Council (LBBC) and the Arab Bankers Association (ABA) have signed a Memorandum of Collaboration to strengthen engagement across the UK, Libya and the wider Arab banking and financial community.

On 23 September, LBBC Chairman Peter Millett CMG and Gaby Fadel, CEO of the Arab Bankers Association, signed the Memorandum at the British Arab Commercial Bank (BACB) offices in London.

The collaboration will support greater engagement between the two organisations through joint events, member activities and dialogue on Libya’s banking sector, financial reform and investment climate.

The partnership builds on the LBBC’s wider engagement with Libya’s financial sector, including the recent lunch organised alongside the Central Bank of Libya (CBL), which brought together representatives from the banking and financial community for discussions on Libya’s evolving financial landscape.

Through this collaboration, the LBBC and ABA will look to create further opportunities for members to connect, exchange expertise and engage in discussions relevant to the development of Libya’s financial and investment environment.

The LBBC would like to thank BACB plc, an LBBC sponsor, for hosting the signing and looks forward to working with the Arab Bankers Association and its members.

The LBBC looks forward to building on this collaboration and supporting continued dialogue and engagement across the UK-Libya financial and business community.
