The Libyan British Business Council (LBBC) visited the Renewable Energy Authority of Libya (REAOL) as part of its UK Energy Delegation programme, meeting with Chairman Dr. Abdulsalam Alansari and senior representatives of the Authority to discuss the development of Libya’s renewable energy sector.

During the visit, REAOL presented a number of renewable energy projects that are open to investment. The Authority also shared a case study on the investment model being developed to support project bankability and help move projects towards implementation.

The meeting gave delegates the opportunity to hear directly from REAOL about its plans for the sector, ask questions and discuss the role that international companies can play through investment, technical expertise and partnerships.

Representatives from bp, Shell, Glencore, Metlen, KBR, Inc. and John Reid & Sons (ReidSteel) joined the LBBC delegation, bringing experience across energy, trading, engineering and infrastructure.

The visit builds on the LBBC’s wider engagement around renewable energy in Libya, following the Libya Energy Transition Conference held in London last November. It also forms part of the Council’s continued engagement with Libyan institutions and organisations working on the country’s energy transition.

Following the discussions in Tripoli, the LBBC is now planning further engagement in London, giving members the opportunity to continue discussions around specific renewable energy projects and potential areas for technical and commercial cooperation.

The visit provided an opportunity to strengthen relationships with REAOL and gain a clearer understanding of the projects and investment models being developed within Libya’s renewable energy sector. The LBBC will continue to support dialogue between its members and relevant Libyan institutions as these opportunities develop.
