## Opportunity for LBBC Members: Global Competitive Urban Planning Initiative – Benghazi

The Libyan British Business Council is pleased to bring to the attention of its members a significant opportunity to engage with one of Libya’s most important urban development programmes.

The Global Competitive Urban Planning Initiative – Benghazi (GCUPI-B) is a competition led by the Fund for Development and Reconstruction of Libya (FDRL). It invites international companies, planning experts, consortiums and institutions to contribute to the development of a new Master Urban Plan for Benghazi.

As Libya’s second-largest city, Benghazi has substantial development needs across infrastructure, housing, transport and digital systems. For UK companies, the initiative offers a structured route into Libya’s reconstruction and development market, while also creating the opportunity to build relationships with key Libyan decision-makers and position early for future commercial opportunities.

Participation follows a clear three-stage process. Interested contributors are invited to register by **31 July 2026**, after which they will receive the full initiative guidelines and the current plans for the city. A concept note contributing to the Master Urban Plan is then due by 31 August 2026. The process will conclude with a final three-day event in Benghazi from 6–8 September 2026, featuring a conference on the initiative’s objectives, an exhibition of submitted concept projects, and B2B meetings alongside the announcement of results.

The LBBC will attend the final event in September and is well placed to support member companies throughout the process. This includes background information on the initiative and the FDRL, guidance on registration and participation, hotel reservation support in Benghazi, and facilitation of meetings and introductions to relevant contacts and decision-makers.
